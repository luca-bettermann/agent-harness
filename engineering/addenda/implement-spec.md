## Our additions

- **Builder:** an implementer is an ordinary subagent started with exactly `Implement <issue>.`; anything run-specific goes on the issue as a comment. It fetches the issue with its comments, reads `<workspace>/.agent-harness/skills/implement/SKILL.md` and follows it except its final `code-review` (step 7 reviews the project once), and ends with the report below.
- **Report:** each implementer ends with a comment on its issue under five headings: *Spec* (each item and each test that must fail on the old code, with where it is covered), *Rulings* (each operator ruling on the issue and its project, and how it is honoured), *Principles* (AGENTS principles and the duties of the skills it loaded, such as `frontend` screenshots and the browser walk), *Deviations* (anything done differently from the spec, and why), *CI* (one row per job: affected, reason, pipeline/job ID or "not run: unaffected"; a red-first proof cites a local command).
- **Report check:** before step 5 merges an issue, the orchestrator reads that report against the issue, the project's rulings and the skills the work required. A missing or unconvincing heading is never relayed as done: the issue goes back to an implementer with the gap stated, or the gap becomes a question to the operator. This checks intent and process per issue; the code itself is checked by step 7's `code-review`.
- **Checkpoints:** steps 1 and 9 are the project's self-correction checkpoints (AGENTS): compare branches, worktrees and scratch folders with the tracker, remove those whose issue is closed, and report those that name no issue.
