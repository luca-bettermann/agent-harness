## Our additions

- **Issues:** load the installation's `issue-tracker` skill for acceptance criteria and work-package size; these replace the sizing in the vertical-slice rules above.
- **Knowledge issue:** every project ends with one issue "Update knowledge", blocked by all other issues and created as `ready-for-human`; `implement-spec` gives it to no implementer, and it is the one exception to vertical slices. After the final `code-review`, the operator runs it in an interactive session: it walks the knowledge update triggers for the whole project and ends with an accepted promotion, or a sourced reason that no knowledge changes.
