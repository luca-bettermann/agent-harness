---
name: ingest
description: Ingest a note or source into its knowledge module, and turn its action items into an issue. Use when the user says "ingest" about a meeting note, daily note, paper or other file.
---

1. Read the module's `CLAUDE.md` and the `SCHEMA.md` it imports; follow Operations → Ingest and the update triggers.
2. **Action items:** create the issues the trigger asks for right away, in the owning team from INSTANCE, and replace the checkboxes in the note with the issue link. Ask only when the team or owner is unclear.
3. **Pages:** draft the changes the note implies and show them to the module's owner as a promotion.
4. Commit the note. Commit the page changes only once the owner accepts them; otherwise leave them as a proposal. Report the issues and the pages.
